Vorarlberg Mountain Passes: Frequently Asked Questions

Are Vorarlberg mountain pass driving conditions updated live? ▼
Yes. LiveRoadStatus updates road surface status, winter equipment laws, and avalanche closure alerts for all 12 Vorarlberg mountain passes every 5 minutes using official telemetry from ÖAMTC and ASFINAG.
When does the Silvretta High Alpine Road (Bielerhöhe) open and close? ▼
The Silvretta High Alpine Road (2,032 m / L188) is a private toll road that closes for winter from early November to late May due to extreme alpine snowpack. In winter, access to Bielerhöhe from Montafon is provided via cableway and tunnel bus for ski touring.
What are the legal winter tire and snow chain mandates in Austria? ▼
In Austria, situational winter equipment is legally mandatory between November 1 and April 15 whenever roads are covered with snow, slush, or ice. Winter tires must have M+S or 3PMSF markings with a minimum tread depth of 4 mm for radials. Snow chains must be fitted on driven wheels when indicated by official road signs.
Is Flexen Pass (B198) open when the Arlberg Tunnel (S16) is closed? ▼
Flexen Pass (1,773 m) provides the sole road link to Zürs and Lech from the Arlberg Pass road (B197). It features heavy concrete avalanche galleries and is maintained open year-round, though snow chain mandates are frequently enforced during severe winter storms.
