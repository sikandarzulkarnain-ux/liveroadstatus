Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026 • Effective immediately for all visitors

1. Introduction & Overview

At LiveRoadStatus ("we," "our," or "us"), privacy and data minimization are fundamental principles. LiveRoadStatus is a Himalayan-born road intelligence platform dedicated to bringing public mountain pass information into one transparent, accessible location for travelers worldwide.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, process, and protect information when you access our website (liveroadstatus.com) and associated services. We do not sell your personal data or track your activity across unrelated third-party websites.

Key Summary: LiveRoadStatus is designed as an open-access public resource. We do not require registration, personal account creation, or mandatory user data collection to access mountain pass road condition reports.

2. Information We Collect

Because LiveRoadStatus does not require user registration or login credentials, the information we process is strictly technical and navigational:

  • Automated Server Log Data: When you access our website, our servers automatically log basic browser information, including IP address, access timestamp, browser type, operating system, referring URL, and pages requested. This data is used solely for infrastructure diagnostics, DDoS protection, and security monitoring.
  • Aggregated Usage Analytics: We process anonymized analytics data (such as pass page views and regional search traffic) to evaluate which mountain passes require more frequent research attention and system capacity.
  • User Contributions & Feedback: If you voluntarily contact our team or submit road condition updates via email or official feedback channels, we retain your communication history to verify reports and correspond with you.

3. How We Use Information

We use collected technical data strictly for legitimate operational purposes:

  • To serve pass status reports, weather information, and route context efficiently to your device.
  • To detect, prevent, and respond to technical glitches, malicious traffic, or service outages.
  • To improve website performance, mobile responsiveness, and page layout.
  • To verify crowd-sourced road condition reports submitted by researchers and local travelers.

4. Cookies & Web Storage

LiveRoadStatus keeps cookie usage to an absolute minimum:

  • Essential Session Storage: We use local browser storage to remember user interface preferences (such as selected region filters or search state) during your session.
  • No Third-Party Advertising Cookies: LiveRoadStatus does not host invasive third-party ad retargeting networks or persistent cross-site tracking scripts.

5. Information Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, trade, or monetize personal information. Data may only be shared under the following limited circumstances:

  • Infrastructure Service Providers: Trusted hosting, Content Delivery Networks (CDNs), and cloud security providers that store or transfer technical web server traffic under strict confidentiality obligations.
  • Legal Requirements: If required by valid law enforcement requests, court orders, or applicable regulations to protect public safety or platform integrity.

6. Third-Party External Links

Our pass detail pages frequently link to external third-party sources, including official road authority portals, weather services, regional transport departments, and news outlets. LiveRoadStatus is not responsible for the privacy practices, content, or cookie policies of external websites. We encourage visitors to review the privacy notices of any third-party website they visit.

7. Data Security & Retention

We implement standard technical, organizational, and physical security measures to safeguard server infrastructure against unauthorized access, alteration, or data loss. Technical log files are retained for standard operational cycles (typically 30 to 90 days) before automatic purge or anonymization.

8. International Data Transfers & User Rights (GDPR / CCPA)

LiveRoadStatus serves global travelers. Regardless of your location, you hold specific rights under data protection laws such as the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA):

  • Right to Access: Request details regarding any personal data we hold about you (e.g. direct email communications).
  • Right to Erasure: Request the deletion of your personal communications or submitted feedback.
  • Right to Object: Object to processing of technical data where applicable.

9. Updates to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect platform evolution, technological updates, or regulatory adjustments. Revisions will be published on this page with an updated "Last Updated" timestamp.
