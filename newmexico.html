New Mexico Winter Driving & High-Wind Travel Regulations (NMDOT)

During winter snowstorms and spring wind events, New Mexico Department of Transportation (NMDOT) enforces travel advisories, tire chain laws, and high-wind truck warnings across interstate and high-altitude mountain corridors.

Tire Chain & Traction Requirements

During severe winter storms on Raton Pass (I-25), Tijeras Canyon (I-40), and Wilkerson Pass (US-64), NMDOT mandates snow tires or tire chains on commercial trucks and non-4WD vehicles.

Guadalupe Pass High Wind Warnings

US-62/180 through Guadalupe Pass frequently experiences severe crosswinds exceeding 65 mph. High-profile vehicles, light trailers, and RVs are advised to delay travel during red flag wind warnings.
