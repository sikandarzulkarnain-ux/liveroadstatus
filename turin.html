Turin & Susa Valley Passes: Frequently Asked Questions

Is Sestriere Pass open today in Metropolitan City of Turin? ▼
Yes. Sestriere Pass (2,035 m / SR23) is maintained open 365 days a year for ski resort visitors and regional traffic. Snow chains or 4WD are mandatory during winter storms.
Is Colle delle Finestre paved all the way? ▼
No. Colle delle Finestre (2,178 m) is paved on the lower switchbacks from Meana di Susa, but the upper 7.8 kilometers to the pass summit are unpaved gravel, closed during winter.
How high is Colle del Sommeiller? ▼
Colle del Sommeiller reaches an altitude of 3,009 meters (9,872 ft) above Bardonecchia, making it the highest driveable unpaved mountain pass road in Europe.
