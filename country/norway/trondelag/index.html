Storlien (Norway – Sweden Border)

E14 • Meråker to Storlien / Åre
• OPEN
Elevation: 600 m • Region: Meråker / Storlien

Essential year-round international mountain crossing linking Stjørdal & Trondheim to Sweden (Åre). Continuous winter clearing; convoy driving during severe blizzards.

Rørosfjellet

Fv30 / Fv705 • Røros Plateau
• OPEN
Elevation: 750 m – 900 m • Region: Røros Region

High mountain plateau surrounding UNESCO World Heritage town Røros. Connects eastern Norway to Swedish border area. Sub-zero temperatures and drift snow in winter.

Stugudalen – Brekken

Fv705 • Røros to Brekken & Tydal
• OPEN
Elevation: 850 m • Region: Brekken / Tydal

High mountain plateau road connecting Røros, Brekken, and Stugudalen. Exposed to wind drifts across open mountain tundra.
