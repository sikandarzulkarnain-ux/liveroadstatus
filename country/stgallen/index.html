St. Gallen & Appenzell Passes: Frequently Asked Questions

Are Hulftegg Pass and Wasserfluh Pass monitored by TCS? ▼
Yes. Touring Club Schweiz (TCS) explicitly lists both Hulfteggpass (953 m) and Wasserfluhpass (843 m) among its official monitored road passes in Switzerland, tracking winter road surface conditions and traffic updates.
Is Schwägalp Pass open year-round? ▼
Yes. Schwägalp Pass (1,278 m) at the base of Mount Säntis is maintained open 365 days a year for commuters and Säntis cableway visitors, with active snowplow clearance.
Where can I check live TCS pass road conditions for St. Gallen & Appenzell? ▼
LiveRoadStatus updates real-time driving conditions directly from TCS (Touring Club Schweiz) at tcs.ch and Alpenpässe.ch.
