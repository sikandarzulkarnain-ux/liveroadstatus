Frequently Asked Questions (Arunachal Pass Status)

Direct verified answers for Arunachal Pradesh mountain pass road conditions, live open/closed status, and Sela Tunnel advisories.

Sela Pass / Se La (elevation 13,700 ft / 4,170 m) on NH-13 Trans-Arunachal Highway is OPEN year-round. The newly constructed twin-tube 12 km Sela Tunnel provides all-weather connectivity bypassing winter snow drifts. BRO Project Vartak maintains active snow plowing over the high summit pass.

Bum La Pass (elevation 15,200 ft / 4,633 m) on the Indo-China LAC border 37 km north of Tawang is RESTRICTED. Open for domestic Indian visitors who hold valid Inner Line Permits (ILP) and special army passes from Tawang Brigade & District Magistrate.

Pangsau Pass (elevation 3,727 ft / 1,136 m) on WWII Stilwell Road (Ledo Road) in Changlang district is OPEN. Sourced live from BRO Project Udayak and Assam Rifles.

Diphu Pass / Dipher Pass (15,049 ft) in Anjaw district is a RESTRICTED strategic tri-junction border pass where India, China, and Myanmar borders intersect. Accessible to Indian Armed Forces & ITBP patrols.
