Canton Valais / Wallis Passes: Frequently Asked Questions

Which mountain passes in Valais are open all year? ▼
Simplon Pass (2,005 m) on A9, Col de la Forclaz (1,527 m) on Route 21, and Col de Morgins (1,371 m) are open year-round with continuous snowplowing. The Great St Bernard Tunnel provides 365-day access underneath Great St Bernard Pass.
When do high passes like Furka, Nufenen, and Great St Bernard open? ▼
High passes above 2,400 m (Furkapass, Nufenenpass, Grand-Saint-Bernard) typically open in late May or early June after snowplow crews clear 10-meter snowdrifts. They remain open through October until the first heavy autumn snowfall.
Do I need a Swiss motorway vignette to cross Simplon Pass? ▼
No, the Simplon Pass road (A9) between Brig and Iselle is exempt from the Swiss motorway sticker (vignette) requirement, allowing free travel for passenger cars.
Where can I check live TCS pass road conditions? ▼
LiveRoadStatus updates real-time driving conditions directly from TCS (Touring Club Schweiz) at tcs.ch and alpenpaesse.ch.
