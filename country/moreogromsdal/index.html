Trollstigen (Troll's Ladder)

Fv63 • Valldal to Åndalsnes
• OPEN
Elevation: 858 m • Region: Geiranger / Trollstigen

Norway's iconic hairpin mountain pass featuring 11 tight hairpin bends and Stigfossen waterfall bridge. Seasonal closure June–October.

Geirangerfjellet

Fv63 • Geiranger to Langvatn / Grotli
• OPEN
Elevation: 1,476 m • Region: Geiranger Plateau

Panoramic mountain pass connecting Geirangerfjord to Grotli and Dalsnibba Skywalk (1,500 m). High alpine terrain with heavy winter snowfall.

Ørnevegen (Eagle Road)

Fv63 • Geiranger to Eidsdal
• OPEN
Elevation: 620 m • Region: Geirangerfjord

Famous Eagle Bend highway ascending steeply from Geiranger village to Ørnesvingen viewpoint overlooking Seven Sisters waterfall.

Aursjøvegen

Toll Road • Eikesdal to Sunndalsøra
• OPEN
Elevation: 1,060 m • Region: Romsdal / Sunndal

Wild, high-altitude gravel road across the Aursjøen dam plateau. Features hairpins carved into vertical canyon walls. Open June–October.

Ørskogfjellet

E39 • Sjøholt to Vestnes
• OPEN
Elevation: 870 m • Region: Sunnmøre

Major trunk highway pass on E39 connecting Ålesund & Sunnmøre with Molde/Vestnes. Maintained 24/7 with active snowplow fleets.

Norangsdalen

Fv655 • Øye to Hellesylt
• OPEN
Elevation: 905 m • Region: Sunnmøre Alps

One of Norway's narrowest mountain pass canyons, passing submerged 1908 village ruins in Lake Lyngstøylvatnet. High avalanche walls.

Eikesdal & Sunndal Pass

Fv6012 • Eidsvåg to Mardalsfossen
• OPEN
Elevation: 930 m • Region: Romsdal / Eikesdal

Scenic fjord-and-mountain valley road alongside Eikesdalsvatnet leading to Mardalsfossen (one of Europe's highest waterfalls).
