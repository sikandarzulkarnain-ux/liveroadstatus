Yukon Territory Mountain Passes: Frequently Asked Questions

What are travel advisories for the Dempster Highway (North Fork Pass & Windy Pass)? ▼
The Dempster Highway (Hwy 5) is Canada's only all-weather road crossing the Arctic Circle. Passing through Windy Pass (1,060 m) and North Fork Pass (1,280 m) in Tombstone Territorial Park, drivers encounter unpaved gravel, sharp shale hazards, and intense winter ground blizzards. Two full-size spare tires, emergency satellite communications, and winter severe snow tires are essential year-round.
Are White Pass and Chilkat Pass border crossings open year-round? ▼
Yes, White Pass (South Klondike Highway 2) and Chilkat Pass (Haines Highway 3) are maintained year-round by Yukon Highways and Alaska DOT. Severe arctic blizzards and high winds can cause temporary safety closures up to 24 hours. Valid passports are required for border crossings between Skagway/Haines (AK) and Yukon.
Is Canol Road (Macmillan Pass & Quiet Lake Summit) maintained in winter? ▼
No, the North and South Canol Road (Highway 6) is an unpaved wilderness route that is not maintained or snowplowed in winter. South Canol Road closes seasonally between October and late May, while North Canol Road past Ross River to Macmillan Pass is an extreme wilderness track requiring 4x4 vehicles.
Where can I check live 511 Yukon highway status updates? ▼
LiveRoadStatus updates live road condition advisories directly from 511 Yukon (511yukon.ca), Alaska DOT&PF, and Parks Canada for White Pass, Chilkat Pass, the Alaska Highway, and Dempster Highway passes.
