Salzburg Mountain Passes & Großglockner: Frequently Asked Questions

How can I check Großglockner road conditions today and webcam streams? ▼
LiveRoadStatus provides live Großglockner road conditions today, HD webcams from Edelweißspitze and Fuscher Törl, snow depth measurements, and weather forecasts updated every 5 minutes directly from GROHAG (Großglockner Hochalpenstraßen AG) and ÖAMTC.
What are the opening and closing dates for Großglockner High Alpine Road? ▼
The Großglockner High Alpine Road typically has an opening date in early May (after intensive snowblower clearance of 10m+ snowdrifts) and a closing date in late October or early November due to winter snowpack.
Where can I check Gerlos Pass webcam, weather, and snow conditions? ▼
Gerlos Pass (1,531 m) on B165 connects Krimml with Zillertal. LiveRoadStatus monitors Gerlos Pass open today status, live webcam views of the Krimml toll plaza, snow chain laws, and current weather.
What are Pass Thurn road conditions today and winter rules? ▼
Pass Thurn (1,274 m) on B161 is maintained open 365 days a year between Mittersill and Kitzbühel. Between Nov 1 and Apr 15, winter tires (≥4mm tread) are mandatory under winter road conditions.
What are the elevation and road conditions at Fuscher Törl and Edelweißspitze? ▼
Edelweißspitze (2,571 m) is the highest vehicle summit on the Großglockner road, accessed via a paved cobblestone spur. Fuscher Törl (2,428 m) sits on the main B107 route. Both feature HD webcam feeds, real-time weather, and winter closure tracking.
