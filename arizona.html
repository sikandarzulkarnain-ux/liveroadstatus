Arizona Mountain Passes: Frequently Asked Questions

Direct verified answers for Arizona pass road conditions, winter closures, and ADOT status updates.

Salt River Canyon on US-60 between Globe and Show Low is OPEN year-round. ADOT plows maintain the winding 6% grades across the Mogollon Rim transition during winter snow squalls.

Oak Creek Canyon (AZ-89A) between Sedona and Flagstaff is OPEN. Switchbacks feature sharp 15 mph curves; ADOT mandates snow chains or traction tires during winter freezes.

AZ-67 to the Grand Canyon North Rim is closed seasonally in winter from December 1 to May 15 due to heavy snow accumulation at 8,000+ feet.

Mt. Lemmon Highway (Catalina Highway) is OPEN. ADOT and Pima County Sheriff enforce 4WD/chains only at Molino Basin during winter snowstorms.
