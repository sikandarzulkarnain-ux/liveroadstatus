Frequently Asked Questions (Sikkim Pass Status)

Direct verified answers for Sikkim high mountain pass road conditions, live open/closed status, and PAP permit requirements.

Nathu La (elevation 14,140 ft / 4,310 m) in East Sikkim is OPEN Wednesday through Sunday for Indian citizens holding valid Protected Area Permits (PAP). Issued by Sikkim Tourism / Gangtok Police. Jawaharlal Nehru Road is kept clear of winter snow continuously by BRO Project Swastik blowers. Closed Mondays and Tuesdays for road maintenance.

Jelep La (elevation 13,999 ft / 4,267 m) near Baba Mandir and Kupup Lake is RESTRICTED. Sourced live from BRO Project Swastik and Indian Army border control.

Goecha La / Goecha Pass (elevation 16,207 ft / 4,940 m) in West Sikkim is OPEN during spring (March to May) and autumn (September to November). Managed by Kanchenjunga National Park with registered guide teams starting from Yuksom.

Dongkha La (Donkia Pass) (18,156 ft) and Naku La (17,322 ft) in North Sikkim are RESTRICTED military border zones under Indian Army and ITBP jurisdiction near the LAC.
