Frequently Asked Questions (Nagaland Pass Status)

Direct verified answers for Nagaland mountain pass road conditions, live open/closed status, and Inner Line Permit (ILP) rules.

Japfü Pass (elevation 10,000 ft / 3,048 m) and Tsepi Pass (8,858 ft / 2,700 m) in Kohima district are OPEN. They serve as the chief mountain trail approaches to Dzüko Valley, maintained by Nagaland Tourism and local Southern Angami village councils.

Pangsha Pass (elevation 6,988 ft / 2,130 m) in Noklak district on the Myanmar border is OPEN. Sourced live from BRO Project Sewak and Assam Rifles at International Trade Centre (ITC) Dan.

Naga Hills ridge passes across Kohima, Tuensang, Mokokchung, Phek, and Kiphire highways are OPEN. Maintained by BRO Project Sewak and Nagaland PWD.
