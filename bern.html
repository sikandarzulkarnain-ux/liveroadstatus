Canton Bern Passes: Frequently Asked Questions

When do Grimsel Pass and Susten Pass close for winter? ▼
Grimselpass (2,164 m) and Sustenpass (2,264 m) typically close for the winter season in late October or early November depending on first heavy alpine snowfalls, reopening in late May or early June after snow clearing.
Is Brünig Pass kept open year-round? ▼
Yes, Brünigpass (1,008 m) on Hauptstrasse 4 is kept open 365 days a year with continuous snowplowing service between Meiringen and Lucerne.
Are private vehicles permitted over Grosse Scheidegg? ▼
No, Grosse Scheidegg is closed to private motorized cars during the summer season. Only yellow Swiss PostBuses, local farm vehicles, cyclists, and hikers are permitted to travel over the pass.
Where can I check official live Swiss mountain pass status? ▼
LiveRoadStatus syncs live road status directly from TCS (Touring Club Schweiz) and Alpenpässe.ch, managed by Canton Tiefbauämter.
