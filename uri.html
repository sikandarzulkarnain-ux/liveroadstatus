Canton Uri Passes: Frequently Asked Questions

When does Gotthard Pass close for winter? ▼
The Gotthard Pass road (2,106 m) typically closes for winter in early November due to severe snowpack, reopening in late May. The Gotthard Road Tunnel (A2 motorway) remains open 365 days a year.
What is the Tremola road on Gotthard Pass? ▼
The Tremola is the historical granite cobblestone road climbing the southern ramp of Gotthard Pass into Airolo, featuring 24 sharp hairpin turns and protected as a Swiss national monument.
