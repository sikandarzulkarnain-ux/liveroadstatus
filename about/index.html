About LiveRoadStatus

The road intelligence platform for mountain passes around the world.

LiveRoadStatus is a Himalayan-born startup founded by Zulkarnain, a native of the Himalayas, with a simple goal: make mountain-pass road information easier to find, understand, and use before a journey.

The Challenge on Mountain Roads

Mountain roads can change quickly. A pass that is open in the morning may face snowfall, restrictions, closures, or difficult driving conditions later in the day. Information about these changes is often scattered across government notices, local news, weather reports, road authorities, social media, and regional sources.

Bringing Scattered Signals Together

LiveRoadStatus brings these signals together in one place. By structuring raw field data and official bulletins into clear, standardized status feeds, we help drivers, motorcyclists, and mountain travelers make informed decisions.

Whether you are navigating the high Himalayan passes of Ladakh, the alpine switchbacks of the Swiss Alps, or the rugged gaps of the American Rockies, LiveRoadStatus gives you a single source of truth.

How We Collect Information

Our research team monitors information from a wide range of publicly available sources to keep mountain pass data accurate and actionable:

🏛️
Official Announcements
Official road authority, police bulletin, and government transportation notices (BRO, CDOT, TCS, Parks Canada).
📰
Local News Reports
Verified news updates from regional journalists and local press reporting on mountain road closures and landslides.
❄️
Weather & Snowfall Data
Real-time satellite weather tracking, freeze-thaw cycles, and seasonal snowpack measurements.
📱
Public Social Media
Publicly available eyewitness reports and driver posts shared by travellers crossing high passes.
🛣️
Travel & Road Reports
Field transit logs, convoy schedules, and Inner Line Permit (ILP) checkpost updates.
📍
Local Regional Sources
Direct feedback from mountain guides, village councils, taxi unions, and local mountain communities.

🤖 AI-Assisted Processing & Human Review

We use AI-assisted technologies and automated data collection tools to help discover, organize, and process information at scale. Our team then reviews the information and updates the relevant pass pages.

Update Cadence: Our goal is to update pass information approximately every 24 hours, while prioritizing more frequent attention when significant changes are reported.

Built from the Himalayas, Designed for the World

LiveRoadStatus was inspired by the realities of Himalayan travel, where road conditions, snowfall, landslides, seasonal closures, and rapidly changing weather can have a major impact on journeys.

What started with an interest in Himalayan mountain roads is being developed into a global mountain-pass information platform.

We are working toward covering mountain passes across Asia, Europe, North America, South America, Africa, and other mountainous regions, giving travelers one place to research road conditions before heading into the mountains.

⚠️ Transparency Matters

LiveRoadStatus is an information and research platform. Road conditions can change rapidly, and information from third-party sources may sometimes be delayed, incomplete, or incorrect.

For critical travel decisions, users should always check the latest information from the relevant road authority, government agency, or local authority before travelling.

We do not claim that every update represents real-time conditions at the exact location of a pass. Our aim is to make the latest available information easier to discover, compare, and understand.
