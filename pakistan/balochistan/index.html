Frequently Asked Questions & Travel Advisories

Bolan Pass (elevation 5,882 ft / 1,793 m) on N-65 Highway connecting Sibi and Jacobabad to Quetta is OPEN. Paved 2-lane National Highway maintained by NHA; open for heavy commercial trucks, buses, and passenger cars. Travelers should monitor monsoon weather advisories due to occasional seasonal flash flood warnings near Dozan and Mach gorges.

Khojak Pass (elevation 7,513 ft / 2,290 m) on N-25 Highway across the Toba Kakar Range connecting Quetta/Qila Abdullah to Chaman (Afghan border) is OPEN. Paved mountain highway; NHA operates snow plows near the summit during winter snowstorms.

Lak Pass (elevation ~7,382 ft / ~2,250 m) on N-25 Highway south of Quetta toward Mastung and Kalat is OPEN. Smooth paved 2-lane road maintained year-round by National Highway Authority (NHA) and patrolled by Motorway Police (NHMP).

Harnai Pass (elevation ~4,986 ft / ~1,520 m) connecting Harnai to Ziarat is OPEN. Paved mountain road; caution advised for steep cliff-side switchbacks.

Mula Pass / Moola Pass (elevation ~4,035 ft / ~1,230 m) in Khuzdar District is OPEN for 4WD jeeps and local mountain traffic. Famous for Moola Chotok gorge waterfalls and sandstone canyons.

Gonshero Pass (elevation ~3,608 ft / ~1,100 m) on N-40 Highway in Chagai District near Taftan (Iran border) is OPEN. Clear paved international highway stretch.
