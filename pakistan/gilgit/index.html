Frequently Asked Questions & Road Condition Advisories

Khunjerab Pass (elevation 15,397 ft / 4,693 m) on the Karakoram Highway (KKH NH-35) is OPEN during seasonal border operational windows (typically April 1 to November 30 for tourist passenger traffic, and open year-round for commercial container shipping under official snow-clearance protocols). National Highway Authority (NHA) & Chinese Highway Authorities operate continuous snow plows. High altitude oxygen levels are around 55% of sea level; drivers should prepare for extreme cold and high-wind conditions near the summit gate.

Kilik Pass (elevation 15,430 ft / 4,703 m) in Upper Hunza (Gojal) near the Afghan Wakhan Corridor is RESTRICTED. While open for licensed trekking expeditions during summer (June to September), visitors must obtain non-objection certificates (NOC) and border security clearances from Gilgit-Baltistan administration due to its proximity to international borders.

Mintaka Pass (elevation 15,420 ft / 4,700 m) is RESTRICTED. This ancient Silk Road caravan route into Xinjiang, China is closed to standard commercial and passenger traffic. Special permits issued by the Gilgit-Baltistan Home Department and Pakistan Military are required for any high-altitude research or exploration teams.

Shimshal Pass (elevation 15,535 ft / 4,735 m) in Shimshal Valley, Hunza is OPEN for summer pasturing and alpine trekking (May to October). Access requires navigating the 53 km unpaved 4WD Shimshal Valley jeep road from Pasu, followed by a 2-day trek to the high Pamir pastures. Drivers on the jeep road should exercise extreme caution near steep gravel slides and narrow canyon ledges.

Chapursan Pass (elevation 15,814 ft / 4,820 m) in Gojal, Upper Hunza is OPEN via high-clearance 4WD jeep track up to Baba Ghundi Ziarat. High pass trails beyond Baba Ghundi leading toward Wakhan are open for summer trekking with local Wakhi mountain guides from June to late September.

Irshad Pass (elevation 15,059 ft / 4,590 m) connecting Chapursan Valley to the Wakhan Corridor in Afghanistan is RESTRICTED. Strictly accessible to approved mountaineering and border exploration parties carrying Ministry of Interior / GB Home Dept permits.

Lupghar Pir Pass (elevation 15,912 ft / 4,850 m) in the Shimshal Karakoram is RESTRICTED / EXPEDITION ONLY. Severe alpine glacier terrain requiring crampons, ice axes, and registered climbing guides.

Chaprot Pass (elevation 16,076 ft / 4,900 m) traversing the boundary between Hunza and Nagar districts is OPEN for seasonal summer alpine trekking from mid-June to early October.

Daintar Pass (elevation 15,545 ft / 4,738 m) in Daintar Nala is OPEN for alpine summer pasture treks. Unpaved trail accessible with local mountain guides.

Hayal Pass (elevation 15,256 ft / 4,650 m) linking Naltar Valley to Hunza is OPEN for alpine summer trekking between July and September.

Shandur Pass (elevation 12,139 ft / 3,700 m) connecting Ghizer (GB) to Chitral (KP) is OPEN from May through November. Famous for the annual Shandur Polo Festival held every July at the world's highest polo ground. Unpaved gravel road suitable for 4WD vehicles and high clearance SUVs.

Hispar Pass (elevation 16,824 ft / 5,128 m) and Hispar La (5,152 m) on the Biafo-Hispar Snow Lake traverse are OPEN for technical glacier trekking from late June through early September. Requires licensed Karakoram mountain guides, porters, and full glacier safety gear.

Gondogoro Pass (Gondogoro La) (elevation 18,323 ft / 5,585 m) connecting K2 Base Camp / Concordia on Baltoro Glacier to Hushe Valley in Baltistan is OPEN from mid-June to early September. High altitude technical glacier pass with fixed ropes managed by Rescue 1122 and local guide associations.

Burzil Pass (elevation 13,451 ft / 4,100 m) in southern Astore District is RESTRICTED / CLOSED to civilian tourist traffic due to its location along the sensitive Line of Control (LOC) border zone. Military permits are required.

Deosai Area Passes (elevation ~13,451 ft / 4,100 m) across Deosai National Park linking Skardu to Astore are OPEN during summer (June to October). 4WD vehicles recommended for unpaved plateau tracks; closed during winter heavy snowpack (Nov-May).
