Frequently Asked Questions & Travel Advisories

Khyber Pass (elevation 3,510 ft / 1,070 m) on the N-5 Grand Trunk Road connecting Peshawar to Landi Kotal and Torkham border is OPEN. Paved 2-lane dual carriageway highway with continuous freight and passenger transport flow under National Highway Authority (NHA) and Frontier Corps maintenance.

Lowari Pass (elevation 10,230 ft / 3,118 m) surface mountain top road is closed in winter due to severe snowpack, but the Lowari Tunnel (8.5 km) on N-45 Highway is OPEN 24/7, providing all-weather motor connectivity between Dir and Chitral.

Shandur Pass (elevation 12,139 ft / 3,700 m) connecting Chitral to Ghizer (Gilgit-Baltistan) is OPEN from May through November. Unpaved gravel road accessible by 4WD jeeps and SUVs. Site of the annual Shandur Polo Festival every July.

Malakand Pass (elevation 4,593 ft / 1,400 m) on N-45 Highway connecting Mardan and Dargai to Swat and Dir is OPEN year-round. Paved 2-lane highway with smooth engineered bends.

Shangla Pass (elevation 7,000 ft / 2,134 m) connecting Swat Valley (Alpuri) to Besham on the Karakoram Highway (KKH) is OPEN. KP Highway Authority maintains snow clearance and salting in winter.

Malam Jabba Pass (elevation 9,199 ft / 2,804 m) leading to Swat Valley's premier ski resort is OPEN. Continuous snow plowing during ski season; snow chains recommended after fresh snowfall.

Kohat Pass via Darra Adam Khel is OPEN. Main traffic bypasses via the 1.9 km Kohat Tunnel on N-55 Indus Highway; old pass road is open to local traffic.

Badgoi Pass (elevation 11,548 ft / 3,520 m) connecting Kalam (Swat) to Kumrat Valley (Upper Dir) is OPEN June through October for 4WD jeeps. Closed in winter due to heavy snow.

Broghil / Baroghil Pass (elevation 12,460 ft / 3,798 m) in Upper Chitral near the Afghan Wakhan Corridor is OPEN during summer (June to October) for 4WD jeeps and eco-tourists with Broghil National Park permits.
