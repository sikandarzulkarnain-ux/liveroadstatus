Sognefjellet

Fv55 • Lom to Gaupne
• OPEN
Elevation: 1,434 m • Region: Jotunheimen

Norway's highest mountain pass. Monitored for autumn night closures and winter snow accumulation. Mandatory winter equipment in shoulder seasons.

Valdresflye

Fv51 • Beitostølen to Vågå
• OPEN
Elevation: 1,389 m • Region: Jotunheimen / Valdres

Norway's second-highest mountain pass across high alpine tundra. Subject to strong winds, drift snow, and timed convoy driving in early winter.

Jotunheimvegen

Mountain Toll Road • Skåbu to Bygdin
• OPEN
Elevation: 1,184 m • Region: Jotunheimen

Scenic high-altitude gravel road passing Lake Vinstre. Seasonal toll road open for summer and autumn travel; closed during deep winter snow.

Tindevegen

Alpine Toll Road • Årdal to Turtagrø
• OPEN
Elevation: 1,210 m • Region: Jotunheimen / Vestland

Spectacular "Shortcut through the Peaks of Hurrungane" connecting Øvre Årdal to Sognefjellet (Fv55). Toll road open May through November.

Tyin – Eidsbugarden

Fv253 • Tyinkrysset to Eidsbugarden
• OPEN
Elevation: 1,066 m • Region: Jotunheimen / Tyin

High mountain lake access road along Lake Tyin to Eidsbugarden & Fondsbu. Closed to private motor vehicles in winter (snow coach operation).

Tyin – Årdal

Fv53 • Tyinkrysset to Årdalstangen
• OPEN
Elevation: 1,085 m • Region: Valdres / Årdal

High mountain pass linking Filefjell/Tyin plateau to the Årdalsfjord. Features high tunnels and avalanche control gates.

Filefjell

E16 • Vang to Lærdal
• OPEN
Elevation: 1,256 m • Region: Valdres / Lærdal

Norway's principal year-round east-west highway corridor. Modernized with high-altitude snow tunnels to guarantee winter reliability.

Bygdin & Sognefjellshytta Area

Fv51 / Fv55 Summit Zones
• OPEN
Elevation: 1,100 m – 1,400 m • Region: Jotunheimen

High mountain lake crossings around Lake Bygdin and Sognefjellshytta high plateau lodge. High exposure to alpine wind and early snowfall.

Dovrefjell

E6 • Dombås to Hjerkinn / Oppdal
• OPEN
Elevation: 1,014 m • Region: Dovrefjell / Rondane

Major arterial mountain highway E6 connecting Oslo and Trondheim over the Dovrefjell plateau. Year-round operations with snowplow escorts during severe blizzards.
