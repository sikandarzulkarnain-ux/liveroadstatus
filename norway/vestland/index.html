Sognefjellet

Fv55 • Gaupne to Lom
• OPEN
Elevation: 1,434 m • Region: Sogn / Jotunheimen

Northern Europe's highest mountain pass connecting Sognefjord to Lom. Monitored for autumn night closures and winter snowpack accumulation.

Aurlandsfjellet

Fv5627 • Aurland to Lærdal (Snow Road)
• OPEN
Elevation: 1,306 m • Region: Aurland / Lærdal

The famous "Snow Road" (Snøvegen) National Tourist Route overlooking Stegastein view point. Open June–October; closed in winter (Lærdal Tunnel used as year-round alternative).

Vikafjellet

Rv13 • Vik i Sogn to Voss
• OPEN
Elevation: 986 m • Region: Sogn / Voss

Crucial mountain link between Vik i Sogn and Voss. High wind exposure leads to winter convoy driving (kolonnekjøring) during blizzards.

Gaularfjellet

Fv613 • Dragsvik to Eldalsosen
• OPEN
Elevation: 748 m • Region: Sunnfjord / Sogn

National Tourist Route featuring dramatic hairpin turns and Utsikten architecture platform. Closed during winter snow season.

Hol – Aurland

Fv50 • Hol to Vassbygdi / Aurland
• OPEN
Elevation: 1,160 m • Region: Hallingdal / Aurland

High mountain road traversing hydro lakes and narrow tunnels. Year-round alternative route between Hallingdal and Sognefjord.

Tindevegen

Toll Road • Turtagrø to Øvre Årdal
• OPEN
Elevation: 1,210 m • Region: Jotunheimen / Årdal

"Shortcut through the Peaks" connecting Sognefjellet (Fv55) with Årdal. High alpine panorama along Hurrungane peak range.

• OPEN
Elevation: 1,085 m • Region: Valdres / Årdal

High mountain pass linking Tyin plateau to Årdalsfjord. Features mountain tunnels and avalanche control gates.

Frequently Asked Questions — Vestland Passes

When Aurlandsfjellet (Snow Road) is closed for winter, drivers use the Lærdal Tunnel (E16). At 24.5 km, it is the world's longest road tunnel and remains open 24/7 year-round.
Kolonnekjøring (convoy driving) is used by Statens vegvesen during severe winter storms. Vehicles wait at a barrier and follow a heavy snowplow in small escorted convoys across the mountain summit.
Tindevegen is open to cars, motorcycles, and small motorhomes. Heavy vehicles over 10 meters long or with trailers over 2.5 meters wide are discouraged due to narrow hairpin sections.
