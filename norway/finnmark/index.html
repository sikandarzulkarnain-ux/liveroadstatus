Sennalandet

E6 • Alta to Skaidi / Hammerfest
• OPEN
Elevation: 385 m • Region: West Finnmark

Crucial E6 Arctic trunk highway across high tundra plateau between Alta and Skaidi. High risk of snow drift and winter convoy driving (kolonnekjøring).

Hatter

E6 • Skaidi to Olderfjord
• OPEN
Elevation: 250 m • Region: Porsanger / Kvalsund

Arctic highland corridor linking Skaidi with Olderfjord junction. Monitored for sudden sea-fog and icy road surfaces.

Skarsvåg – Nordkapp (North Cape)

E69 • Honningsvåg to Nordkapp Plateau
• OPEN
Elevation: 300 m • Region: Magerøya Island (71°N)

World's northernmost highway leading to the famous North Cape Globe (71°10′21″N). Mandatory convoy driving behind snowplows during winter.

Kvalsund Mountain Passage

Rv94 • Skaidi to Hammerfest
• OPEN
Elevation: 200 m • Region: Hammerfest Peninsula

Arterial coastal mountain route over Kvalsund bridge connecting Hammerfest city with E6 highway network. Exposed to coastal Arctic gales.

Ifjordfjellet

Fv98 • Lakselv to Ifjord / Tana
• OPEN
Elevation: 370 m • Region: East Finnmark Tundra

High Arctic tundra plateau road rebuilt with modern snow barriers. Primary east-west link between Lakselv and Nordkinn/Tana.

Kongsfjordfjellet

Fv890 • Gednje to Berlevåg
• OPEN
Elevation: 320 m • Region: Varanger Peninsula

Isolated Arctic pass providing sole overland access to Berlevåg fishing port. Extreme whiteout conditions during Barents Sea winter storms.

Båtsfjordfjellet

Fv891 • Gednje to Båtsfjord
• OPEN
Elevation: 360 m • Region: Varanger Peninsula

High Arctic plateau pass connecting Varanger tundra with Båtsfjord harbor. Severe winter blizzard environment requiring frequent convoy driving.

Domen Pass

E75 • Kiberg to Vardø Island
• OPEN
Elevation: 230 m • Region: Vardø / East Varanger

Mountain pass over Domen ridge connecting mainland Varanger with the Vardø subsea tunnel. High exposure to Barents Sea coastal gales.

Snefjord – Havøysund

Fv889 • Kokelv to Havøysund
• OPEN
Elevation: 250 m • Region: National Tourist Route

Spectacular coastal Arctic National Tourist Route clinging to cliffs and mountain ridges along the Arctic Ocean.

Bekkarfjord – Hopseidet

Fv888 • Ifjord to Mehamn / Gamvik
• OPEN
Elevation: 300 m • Region: Nordkinn Peninsula

Sole highway corridor connecting the Nordkinn peninsula (Mehamn & Gamvik / Slettnes Lighthouse) across high Arctic tundra.

Hamningbergfjellet

Fv8100 • Vardø to Hamningberg
• OPEN
Elevation: 200 m • Region: Edge of Barents Sea

Surreal moon-like tundra pass winding through jagged rock formations at the edge of the Arctic Ocean. Closed in winter; open June–October.

Vintervollen – Grense Jakobselv

Fv8860 • Kirkenes to Russian Border
• OPEN
Elevation: 220 m • Region: Barents Border Zone

Historic mountain pass route along the Russian border river ending at King Oscar II Chapel on the shore of the Barents Sea.
