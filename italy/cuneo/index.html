Province of Cuneo Passes: Frequently Asked Questions

How high is Colle dell'Agnello (Col Agnel) and is it open today? ▼
Colle dell'Agnello reaches 2,744 meters (9,003 ft), making it the 3rd highest paved mountain pass road in the Alps. The high pass section is closed in winter (typically November to June) due to deep snow accumulation.
Is Colle della Maddalena open year-round? ▼
Yes. Colle della Maddalena (1,996 m / SS21) is a major international transport link between Italy and France maintained open 365 days a year with active snow clearing.
Where can I check live ANAS road conditions for Cuneo passes? ▼
LiveRoadStatus updates real-time driving conditions directly from ANAS S.p.A. (stradeanas.it) and the Province of Cuneo road network authority.
