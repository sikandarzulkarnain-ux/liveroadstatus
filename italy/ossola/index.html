Ossola, Vercelli & Biella Passes: Frequently Asked Questions

Is Simplon Pass / Passo del Sempione open today and where can I watch the webcam? ▼
Yes. Simplon Pass (2,005 m / SS33) is an international highway kept open 365 days a year with active avalanche shelter galleries. Live summit webcams (at the Simplon Eagle monument and Iselle customs) are accessible live on LiveRoadStatus.
What are the driving conditions today on Panoramica Zegna? ▼
The Panoramica Zegna pass road over Bocchetto Sessera (1,373 m) and Bielmonte (1,500 m) in Biella is open year-round with regular snowplow clearance during winter.
Where can I check live ANAS webcams for Northern Piedmont passes? ▼
LiveRoadStatus updates real-time driving conditions directly from ANAS S.p.A. (stradeanas.it) and TCS Switzerland.
