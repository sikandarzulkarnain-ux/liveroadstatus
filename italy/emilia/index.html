Emilia-Romagna Mountain Passes: Frequently Asked Questions

Direct, verified answers for Emilia-Romagna pass road conditions and live open/closed status.

What are Cisa Pass road conditions and is Cisa Pass open or closed? ▼

Cisa Pass (Passo della Cisa, 1,041 m / SS62) is OPEN today. Sourced live from ANAS S.p.A. The historic Via Francigena highway pass connecting Parma with Tuscany is maintained open 365 days a year with active snowplowing. Mandatory winter tires or snow chains are required Nov 15 – Apr 15.

What are Lagastrello Pass road conditions and is Lagastrello Pass open or closed? ▼

Lagastrello Pass (Passo del Lagastrello, 1,200 m / SP665R) is OPEN today. Connecting the Enza Valley in Emilia with Lunigiana in Tuscany, it is maintained open year-round with active snowplow service.

What are Cirone Pass road conditions and is Cirone Pass open or closed? ▼

Cirone Pass (Passo del Cirone, 1,255 m / SP108) is OPEN today. Linking Corniglio in Parma province with Pontremoli in Tuscany, it is open with winter caution for switchback snowpack.

What are Sillara Pass road conditions and is Sillara Pass open or closed? ▼

Sillara Pass (Passo del Sillara, 1,700 m) is an unpaved high mountain ridge trail pass in the Tosco-Emiliano National Park. Accessible for summer hiking and trail trekking; blocked by snow in winter.

What are Borgallo Pass road conditions and is Borgallo Pass open or closed? ▼

Borgallo Pass (Passo del Borgallo, 965 m) is OPEN today. Sourced live from Provincia di Parma. The mountain pass connecting Taro and Magra valleys is open year-round.
