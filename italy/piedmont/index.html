Piedmont Mountain Passes: Frequently Asked Questions

Direct, verified answers for Piedmont pass road conditions and live open/closed status.

What are Sestriere Pass road conditions and is Sestriere Pass open or closed? ▼

Sestriere Pass (Passo del Sestriere, 2,035 m / SR23) is OPEN today. Sourced live from Città Metropolitana di Torino and ANAS. The SR23 highway is open year-round to serve the Sestriere ski resort. Mandatory snow chains or M+S winter tires are required from November 15 through April 15.

What are Colle del Monginevro road conditions and is Colle del Monginevro open or closed? ▼

Colle del Monginevro (Col de Montgenèvre, 1,854 m / SS24) is OPEN today. Sourced live from ANAS S.p.A. and French DIR Centre-Est. Highway SS24 connects Claviere with Briançon and is kept open 365 days a year with active snowplow service.

What are Colle del Moncenisio road conditions and is Colle del Moncenisio open or closed? ▼

Colle del Moncenisio (Col du Mont-Cenis, 2,083 m / SS25) is currently under Winter Closure. The pass road traversing the Mont-Cenis lake plateau closes seasonally during heavy winter snow (typically November to May).

What are Colle delle Finestre road conditions and is Colle delle Finestre open or closed? ▼

Colle delle Finestre (2,178 m / SP172) is under Winter Closure. Famous for its 28 hairpins and 7.8 km unpaved dirt sector featured in the Giro d'Italia, it reopens for driving and cycling in June.

What are Colle del Lys road conditions and is Colle del Lys open or closed? ▼

Colle del Lys (1,311 m / SP197) is OPEN today. Sourced live from Città Metropolitana di Torino. The SP197 paved pass connecting Almese in Val di Susa with Viù in Val di Lanzo remains open year-round.

What are Colle dell'Assietta road conditions and is Colle dell'Assietta open or closed? ▼

Colle dell'Assietta (2,472 m / SP173) is under Winter Closure. This high military dirt ridge road operates seasonally from July 1 to October 31. Motorized vehicle traffic is prohibited on Wednesdays and Saturdays during summer.

What are Colle di Sampeyre road conditions and is Colle di Sampeyre open or closed? ▼

Colle di Sampeyre (2,284 m / SP105) in Cuneo province is under Winter Closure. Connecting Sampeyre (Varaita Valley) with Stroppo (Maira Valley), it typically reopens in June after snow clearing.

What are Colle della Rho road conditions and is Colle della Rho open or closed? ▼

Colle della Rho (Col de la Roue, 2,541 m) is closed by winter snowpack. Located above Bardonecchia, this high alpine border gap is accessible for summer hiking, gravel biking, and enduros.

What are Colle Basset road conditions and is Colle Basset open or closed? ▼

Colle Basset (2,424 m) on the Assietta ridge road between Sestriere and Sauze d'Oulx is under Winter Closure. Reopens in July for high-altitude dirt road driving and mountain biking.

What are Colle del Sommeiller road conditions and is Colle del Sommeiller open or closed? ▼

Colle del Sommeiller (3,009 m) above Bardonecchia is under Winter Closure. Recognized as Europe's highest driveable unpaved mountain pass road, it is open from July to September with a toll fee at Rochemolles (closed to motor vehicles on Thursdays).
