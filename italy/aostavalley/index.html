Valle d'Aosta Passes: Frequently Asked Questions

Is Great St Bernard Pass open today and where is the live webcam? ▼
The high mountain summit road of Great St Bernard Pass (2,469 m) between Italy and Switzerland is closed during winter due to heavy snowpack. However, the Great St Bernard Tunnel (SS27) is open 365 days a year. Live webcam streams at the summit hospice and tunnel entrance are available live on LiveRoadStatus.
Is Little St Bernard Pass open today? ▼
Little St Bernard Pass (2,188 m) connecting La Thuile (Aosta Valley) with Bourg-Saint-Maurice (France) is closed in winter (typically November to late May). Live webcams track snow clearance operations in real-time today.
What are the road conditions today on Colle San Carlo and Colle di Joux? ▼
Colle San Carlo (1,971 m) and Colle di Joux (1,640 m) are maintained open year-round by ANAS highway crews. Winter tires or snow chains are required during active winter snowstorms.
Where can I check live ANAS webcams for Aosta Valley passes? ▼
LiveRoadStatus aggregates real-time road condition data, winter closure notices, and live webcam feeds directly from ANAS S.p.A. (stradeanas.it) and regional Valle d'Aosta transport portals.
