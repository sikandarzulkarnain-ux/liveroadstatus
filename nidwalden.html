Canton Nidwalden Passes: Frequently Asked Questions

Is Ächerli Pass open year-round in Canton Nidwalden? ▼
Yes. Ächerli Pass (1,398 m) connecting Dallenwil (NW) with Kerns (OW) is kept open 365 days a year, though the single-lane road features 15% gradients requiring winter tires or chains during snowstorms.
What are the road/trail conditions for Stanserhorn-area crossings? ▼
The Stanserhorn ridge and Wirzweli plateau are accessible via hiking trails and cableways from spring through autumn.
