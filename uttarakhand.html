Frequently Asked Questions (Uttarakhand Pass & Trek Status)

Direct verified answers for Uttarakhand mountain pass road conditions, live open/closed status, and forest permit requirements.

Mana Pass (Dungri La) (elevation 18,478 ft / 5,632 m) and Niti Pass (16,634 ft) in Chamoli are RESTRICTED border zones. The asphalt motor road constructed by BRO Project Shivalik is open for Indian Armed Forces and ITBP logistics. Special Inner Line Permits (ILP) from the Chamoli District Magistrate are required for civilian visitors.

Lipulekh Pass (elevation 17,500 ft / 5,334 m) in Pithoragarh is RESTRICTED. Sourced live from BRO Project Hirak. The newly widened motor highway connects Dharchula to the pass summit on the Kailash Mansarovar Yatra border route.

Kalindi Pass (Kalindi Khal) (19,521 ft) and Auden's Col (18,012 ft) are technical mountaineering glacier treks. Open for registered expeditions with IMF clearance, certified high-altitude guides, and Uttarakhand Forest permits between May and September.

Kuari Pass (elevation 11,975 ft / 3,650 m) near Joshimath / Auli is OPEN year-round. It is one of Garhwal's most accessible high-altitude meadow treks, offering clear views of Nanda Devi peak.

Lamkhaga Pass (17,336 ft) connecting Harsil to Kinnaur and Sin La Pass (18,028 ft) connecting Darma to Kuthi Valley are OPEN during summer (June to October) for trek teams with valid forest permits and ITBP registration.
