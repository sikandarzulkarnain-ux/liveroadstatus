Nevada Winter Vehicle Chain Law Regulations (NDOT)

Nevada Department of Transportation (NDOT) and Nevada State Police enforce mandatory chain controls on high-elevation Sierra Nevada and Basin & Range passes during winter storm events.

R-1 & R-2: Snow Tires / AWD Controls

Chains required on all vehicles except 4WD/AWD vehicles with snow-tread tires on all four wheels. Required on Mount Rose Summit (NV-431), Spooner Summit (US-50), and Austin Summit during winter storms.

R-3: Maximum Chain Control

Chains or approved traction devices mandatory on ALL vehicles without exception, including 4WD and AWD. Failure to carry chains in designated mountain passes carries state fines up to $1,000.
