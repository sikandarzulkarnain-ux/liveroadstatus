California Mountain Passes: Frequently Asked Questions

Direct verified answers for Sierra Nevada & California pass road conditions, winter gates, and Caltrans chain controls.

Tioga Pass (elevation 9,943 ft / 3,031 m) in Yosemite National Park is OPEN during summer and autumn (late May/June through November). Caltrans and NPS close the pass seasonally in winter due to heavy alpine snowpack.

Sonora Pass (26% steep grade) and Ebbetts Pass are seasonal high Sierra passes open May to November. Caltrans closes both in winter due to extreme snow accumulation and avalanche hazard.

Interstate 80 over Donner Pass (7,239 ft) and US-50 over Echo Summit (7,382 ft) are priority year-round Sierra corridors. Caltrans R1/R2/R3 chain controls are strictly enforced during Sierra snowstorms.

Carson Pass (8,573 ft) is maintained year-round by Caltrans with continuous winter snowplowing. Monitor Pass is closed in winter and reopens each spring.
