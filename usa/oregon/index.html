Oregon Winter Vehicle Chain Law Regulations (ODOT TripCheck)

During winter weather advisories, Oregon Department of Transportation (ODOT) and Oregon State Police enforce mandatory tire chain regulations across all Cascade passes, Siskiyou Summit on I-5, and Deadman Pass on I-84.

Conditional Chain Requirement

Passenger vehicles under 10,000 lbs must carry or use tire chains, or have approved traction tires (M+S or 3-Peak Mountain Snowflake) installed on drive wheels when "Carry Chains or Traction Tires Required" signs are posted.

Commercial Vehicle Minimum Chains

Commercial trucks and vehicles over 10,000 lbs must carry minimum chains and install chains on drive axles when "Chains Required on Commercial Vehicles" is declared on Siskiyou Summit (I-5), Cabbage Hill (I-84), or Santiam Pass (US-20). Fines for violation exceed $880 per offense.
