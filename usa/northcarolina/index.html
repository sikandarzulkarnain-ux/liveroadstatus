North Carolina Winter Driving & Blue Ridge Parkway Weather Gates

North Carolina Department of Transportation (NCDOT / DriveNC) maintains all primary US highways and interstates year-round. High-elevation motor sections of the Blue Ridge Parkway feature physical weather gates closed by National Park Service Rangers during black ice, freezing rain, and winter snowstorms.

US Highway & Interstate Priority Clearing

Primary corridors such as Soco Gap (US-19), Balsam Gap (US-23/74), Deep Gap (US-421), and Swannanoa Gap (I-40) receive continuous 24/7 brine anti-icing sprayers and salt plowing by NCDOT.

Blue Ridge Parkway Weather Closures

Parkway sections over Air Bellows Gap, Bearpen Gap, and Bull Gap are intentionally left unplowed in winter to protect road surfaces, with gates locked whenever freezing fog or ice creates hazardous conditions.
