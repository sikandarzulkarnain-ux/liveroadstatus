Frequently Asked Questions (Verified Pass Status)

Direct verified answers for Colorado mountain pass road conditions, open/closed status, and CDOT traction laws.

Independence Pass (elevation 12,095 ft / 3,687 m) on CO-82 between Aspen and Twin Lakes is OPEN for the summer season. Verified live via CDOT COTrip. Closed seasonally in winter from November through late May due to heavy alpine snowpack.

Loveland Pass (elevation 11,990 ft / 3,655 m) on US-6 is OPEN year-round across the Continental Divide. Verified live via CDOT. Serves as mandatory hazmat bypass for I-70 Eisenhower Tunnel; CDOT Passenger Vehicle Traction Law active during winter storms.

Berthoud Pass (elevation 11,307 ft / 3,447 m) on US-40 connecting Empire to Winter Park is OPEN year-round. Sourced live from CDOT. Maintained 24/7 with active avalanche mitigation in the Stanley Slide zone.

Monarch Pass (elevation 11,312 ft / 3,448 m) on US-50 between Salida and Gunnison is OPEN year-round across the Continental Divide. Verified live via CDOT.

Red Mountain Pass (elevation 11,018 ft / 3,380 m) on US-550 Million Dollar Highway between Ouray and Silverton is OPEN. Verified live via CDOT. High alpine narrow shelf road with steep cliffs; chain laws enforced in winter.

Wolf Creek Pass (elevation 10,857 ft / 3,298 m) on US-160 between Pagosa Springs and South Fork is OPEN year-round. Sourced live from CDOT COTrip. Receives over 430 inches of snow annually; CDOT chain laws strictly enforced during winter fronts.

Trail Ridge Road (elevation 12,183 ft / 3,713 m) on US-34 across Rocky Mountain National Park is OPEN for the summer season (late May to October). Sourced live from NPS and CDOT.

Cottonwood Pass (elevation 12,126 ft / 3,702 m) on CO-306 between Buena Vista and Taylor Park is OPEN for summer travel. Fully paved high Continental Divide crossing; closed in winter.
