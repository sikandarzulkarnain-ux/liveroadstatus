Frequently Asked Questions (Verified Pass Status)

Direct verified answers for Virginia mountain gap road conditions and live open/closed status.

Blue Ridge Gap (elevation 2,280 ft / 695 m) in Bedford and Botetourt Counties, VA on State Route 617 is OPEN. Verified live via VDOT 511. Rural state route pavement is clear. Connecting Blue Ridge Parkway gates nearby are subject to winter ice closures enforced by NPS Rangers.

Cumberland Gap (US Highway 25E Cumberland Gap Tunnel) is OPEN 24/7/365. Sourced live from VDOT 511 and TDOT. The 4-lane twin-bore tunnel bypasses the historic crest between Lee County, VA and Claiborne County, TN / Bell County, KY. Hazardous material cargo trucks must detour via US-58/VA-70.

Fancy Gap (elevation 2,920 ft / 890 m) on Interstate 77 and US-52 in Carroll County, VA is OPEN 24/7/365 with dense fog advisory systems active. Sourced live from VDOT Salem District. As a major 6-lane interstate corridor, VDOT maintains continuous snowplowing and anti-icing. Drivers must monitor VDOT Variable Speed Limit signs due to sudden mountain fog across the escarpment.

The Gap of Dunloe (elevation 790 ft / 241 m) is a famous mountain pass located in County Kerry, Ireland (near Killarney and Black Valley). It is OPEN year-round for pedestrians, cyclists, horse-drawn jaunting cars, and local traffic. Motor vehicles are restricted during peak tourist hours for trail safety.

Jarman Gap (Jarmans Gap) (elevation 2,170 ft / 661 m) at Skyline Drive MP 96.8 and SR-691 in Albemarle/Augusta County, VA is OPEN. Verified live via National Park Service and VDOT. State route approaches are clear, while Skyline Drive sections remain open weather permitting.

Rockfish Gap (elevation 1,900 ft / 579 m) on Interstate 64 and US-250 near Waynesboro/Afton, VA is OPEN 24/7/365. Verified live via VDOT 511. As the major 4-lane trans-mountain Interstate corridor connecting Charlottesville and Staunton, I-64 receives continuous VDOT winter maintenance. It serves as the junction for Skyline Drive MP 105.5 and Blue Ridge Parkway MP 0.

Swift Run Gap (elevation 2,365 ft / 721 m) on US Highway 33 and Skyline Drive MP 65.5 in Rockingham/Greene Counties is OPEN. Sourced live from VDOT Culpeper District. US-33 is a 4-lane divided mountain highway maintained year-round by VDOT plows.

Thornton Gap (elevation 2,304 ft / 702 m) on US Highway 211 and Skyline Drive MP 31.5 near Luray, VA is OPEN. Verified live via VDOT 511. US-211 is a 4-lane divided mountain highway with dry to clear pavement.

Wilson Gap (elevation 1,440 ft / 439 m) on State Route 601 in Loudoun and Clarke Counties, VA near Bluemont is OPEN. Verified live via VDOT NOVA District. 2-lane rural mountain pass road is clear.
