Lombardy Mountain Passes: Frequently Asked Questions

Direct, verified answers for Lombardy pass road conditions and live open/closed status.

What are Stelvio Pass road conditions and is Stelvio Pass open or closed? ▼

Stelvio Pass (Passo dello Stelvio, 2,758 m / SS38) is currently under Winter Closure. Verified live via ANAS S.p.A. and Bolzano Traffic Control. The 2nd highest paved pass in the Alps closes seasonally from early November through late May due to extreme high-altitude snowpack and avalanche risk.

What are Gavia Pass road conditions and is Gavia Pass open or closed? ▼

Gavia Pass (Passo di Gavia, 2,621 m / SP29) is under Winter Closure. Connecting Bormio with Ponte di Legno, this narrow alpine pass features heavy snow accumulation and reopens in early June.

What are Bernina Pass road conditions and is Bernina Pass open or closed? ▼

Bernina Pass (Passo del Bernina, 2,328 m / Highway 29) is OPEN today. Sourced live from TCS Switzerland and ANAS. Providing 365-day access between Engadin / St. Moritz and Tirano in Valtellina, snowplows operate 24/7 during winter storms.

What are Splügen Pass & Spluga Pass road conditions and are they open or closed? ▼

Splügen Pass / Passo dello Spluga (2,115 m / SS36) is under Winter Closure. The international highway pass connecting Chiavenna with Switzerland is closed from November through May.

What are Maloja Pass road conditions and is Maloja Pass open or closed? ▼

Maloja Pass (Passo del Maloja, 1,815 m / SS37 / Highway 3) is OPEN today. Sourced live from TCS Switzerland and ANAS. It connects Val Bregaglia (Chiavenna) with the Upper Engadin valley and St. Moritz with active winter snow clearing.

What are Foscagno Pass road conditions and is Foscagno Pass open or closed? ▼

Foscagno Pass (Passo di Foscagno, 2,291 m / SS301) is OPEN today. Managed live by ANAS S.p.A., it is the primary year-round highway access corridor into the duty-free enclave of Livigno.

What are Forcola di Livigno road conditions and is Forcola di Livigno open or closed? ▼

Forcola di Livigno (Passo della Forcola, 2,315 m / SP32) is under Winter Closure. The direct border pass linking Livigno with Poschiavo / Bernina Pass in Switzerland closes annually from November to May.

What are Umbrail Pass road conditions and is Umbrail Pass open or closed? ▼

Umbrail Pass (Giogo di Santa Maria, 2,501 m / SP80 / Highway 501) is under Winter Closure. The highest paved road pass in Switzerland connects Val Müstair directly to the SS38 Stelvio ramp and opens seasonally from June to October.

What are Aprica Pass road conditions and is Aprica Pass open or closed? ▼

Aprica Pass (Passo dell'Aprica, 1,181 m / SS39) is OPEN today. Sourced live from ANAS Lombardia, it serves as a major commercial and passenger trunk route between Tresenda (Valtellina) and Edolo (Val Camonica).

What are Mortirolo Pass road conditions and is Mortirolo Pass open or closed? ▼

Mortirolo Pass (Passo del Mortirolo, 1,852 m / SP81) is under Winter Closure. Legendary as the steepest climb in Giro d'Italia history, it is closed during winter and reopens for summer driving and cycling in late May.

What are San Marco Pass road conditions and is San Marco Pass open or closed? ▼

San Marco Pass (Passo San Marco, 1,992 m / SP8 / SP9) is under Winter Closure. Connecting Val Brembana in Bergamo with Valtellina in Sondrio, it closes in winter due to snowpack and reopens in late May.

What are Tonale Pass road conditions and is Tonale Pass open or closed? ▼

Tonale Pass (Passo del Tonale, 1,883 m / SS42) is OPEN today. Verified live via ANAS Lombardia and Trentino 24, it connects Ponte di Legno (Val Camonica) with Val di Sole with continuous snowplowing.
