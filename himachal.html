Frequently Asked Questions (Himachal Pass Status)

Direct verified answers for Himachal Pradesh high mountain pass road conditions, live open/closed status, and BRO clearance updates.

Rohtang Pass (elevation 13,058 ft / 3,978 m) is OPEN during summer and autumn (May to November). A Green Tax online permit from the NGT / Kullu administration is mandatory for all non-electric civilian vehicles. Commuters can also utilize the 9.02 km all-weather Atal Tunnel under Rohtang Ridge.

Kunzum Pass / Kunjum Pass (elevation 14,931 ft / 4,551 m) connecting Lahaul Valley to Spiti Valley is OPEN from June to October. Maintained with active annual snow clearing by BRO Project Deepak.

Bara-Lacha La (elevation 16,040 ft / 4,890 m) on NH-3 Leh-Manali Highway is OPEN. BRO Project Deepak maintains continuous snow clearance near Zingzingbar and Suraj Tal.

Jalori Pass (10,236 ft) on NH-305 is OPEN for light motor vehicles and buses. Sach Pass (14,480 ft) linking Chamba to Pangi Valley is OPEN during peak summer/autumn months.

Chandratal Lake Road (elevation 14,100 ft) branching off Batal on NH-505 is OPEN from June to October for SUVs and 4WD vehicles.
