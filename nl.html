Newfoundland and Labrador Passes: Frequently Asked Questions

What are Wreckhouse wind warnings on the Appalachian Trans-Canada Highway? ▼
The Wreckhouse area on Trans-Canada Highway 1 near Port aux Basques experiences localized downslope hurricane-force winds exceeding 150 km/h that blow down off the Long Range Mountains plateau. Environment Canada issues Wreckhouse wind warnings, and NL 511 halts commercial truck traffic for safety.
When is the Gros Morne Mountain Pass Trek closed for ptarmigan nesting? ▼
The Gros Morne Mountain summit trail is closed annually to all hikers from May 1 to late June (typically opening around June 26) to protect nesting rock ptarmigan and fragile alpine vegetation during the spring thawing season.
What permits are required for Torngat Mountains hiking in Labrador? ▼
Expeditions into Torngat Mountains National Park in northern Labrador require mandatory registration with Parks Canada and certified Inuit bear guards or satellite tracking gear due to active polar bear populations.
Where can I check live NL 511 highway driving conditions? ▼
LiveRoadStatus updates live road conditions directly from NL 511 (511nl.ca) managed by the Department of Transportation and Infrastructure.
