Canton Vaud Passes: Frequently Asked Questions

Which Vaud mountain passes are open during winter? ▼
8 out of 9 mountain passes in Canton Vaud are maintained open year-round: Col des Mosses (1,445 m), Col du Pillon (1,546 m), Col de la Givrine (1,228 m), Col du Marchairuz (1,447 m), Col du Mollendruz (1,180 m), Col des Etroits (1,152 m), Col de la Vue des Alpes (1,283 m), and Chalet-à-Gobet (862 m). Only Col de la Croix (1,778 m) closes seasonally in winter.
What are the road conditions for accessing Glacier 3000 via Col du Pillon? ▼
Col du Pillon (1,546 m) is kept open 365 days a year with active snowplow service to ensure continuous access to the Glacier 3000 cable car station. Winter tires are required during snowstorms.
