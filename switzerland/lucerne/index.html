Canton Lucerne Passes: Frequently Asked Questions

Is Glaubenberg Pass open in winter from the Lucerne side? ▼
Glaubenberg Pass (1,543 m) is closed over the high summit pass section between Entlebuch (LU) and Sarnen (OW) in winter.
What are the road conditions on Glaubenbielen Pass (Panoramastrasse)? ▼
Glaubenbielen Pass (1,611 m) along the Panoramastrasse connecting Sörenberg with Giswil is closed during winter, reopening in May after snowplow clearing.
