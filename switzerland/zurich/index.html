Zurich, Zug & Thurgau Passes: Frequently Asked Questions

Are Albis Pass (791 m) and Hulftegg Pass (953 m) listed on TCS? ▼
Yes. Touring Club Schweiz (TCS) explicitly lists Albispass at 791 m and Hulfteggpass at 953 m among its official monitored road passes in Switzerland, providing real-time status updates and snow condition alerts.
Are there mountain passes in Canton Thurgau? ▼
Canton Thurgau has no high alpine passes, but features important prealpine hill pass routes across the Seerücken and Ottenberg (680 m) connecting Weinfelden with Lake Constance.
Where can I check live TCS pass road conditions for Zurich and Zug? ▼
LiveRoadStatus updates real-time driving conditions directly from TCS (Touring Club Schweiz) at tcs.ch and Alpenpässe.ch.
