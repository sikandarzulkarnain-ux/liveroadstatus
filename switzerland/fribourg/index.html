Canton Fribourg / Freiburg Passes: Frequently Asked Questions

Is Jaun Pass (Jaunpass) open during winter? ▼
Jaun Pass / Col du Jaun (1,509 m) is maintained open 365 days a year between Jaun (Fribourg) and Boltigen (Bern), though snow chains or 4WD are frequently required during winter snowstorms.
What are winter driving conditions on Gurnigel Pass? ▼
Gurnigelpass (1,608 m) in Gantrisch Nature Park is generally kept open year-round for access to ski and cross-country areas, though temporary short closures occur during active snowstorms.
