Canton Obwalden Passes: Frequently Asked Questions

Is Brünig Pass open year-round in Canton Obwalden? ▼
Yes. Brünig Pass (1,008 m / 3,307 ft) carrying Hauptstrasse 4 and the A8 motorway is a critical national connection kept open 365 days a year with active snow clearance.
What are the winter road conditions for Glaubenberg Pass? ▼
Glaubenberg Pass (1,543 m) between Sarnen and Entlebuch is closed at the high summit pass section in winter due to heavy snowpack. However, the road from Sarnen up to Langis (cross-country skiing hub) is kept plowed.
Is Glaubenbielen Pass (Panoramastrasse Giswil) open in winter? ▼
No. Glaubenbielen Pass (1,611 m) along the Panoramastrasse from Giswil to Sörenberg is closed throughout winter due to high avalanche risk, usually opening in May.
Can I drive over Ächerli Pass in winter? ▼
Ächerli Pass (1,398 m) connecting Kerns with Dallenwil is open year-round, but it features very narrow lanes and steep 15% inclines. Snow chains or 4WD are required during winter snowfall.
