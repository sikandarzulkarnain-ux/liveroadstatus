Canton Glarus Passes: Frequently Asked Questions

What are the weekend motor vehicle restrictions on Pragel Pass? ▼
Pragelpass (1,550 m) is closed to motor vehicles (cars and motorcycles) on Saturdays and Sundays between Richisau (GL) and Hinterthal (SZ) to protect alpine tranquility. Motorists must use Klausenpass or Hirzel/Sattel highways on weekends.
Is Kerenzerberg Pass open during winter storms? ▼
Yes, Kerenzerbergpass (748 m) above Lake Walen is maintained open 365 days a year with active snowplow service, providing a vital alternative route when the A3 motorway tunnel experiences maintenance or delays.
Where can I check real-time TCS driving conditions for Glarus? ▼
LiveRoadStatus updates live road conditions directly from TCS (Touring Club Schweiz) at tcs.ch and alpenpaesse.ch.
