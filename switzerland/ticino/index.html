Canton Ticino Passes: Frequently Asked Questions

Which mountain passes in Canton Ticino remain open during winter? ▼
Passo del Lucomagno / Lukmanier Pass (1,915 m) and Passo del Monte Ceneri (554 m) are kept open year-round with continuous snowplowing. The Gotthard Tunnel (A2) and San Bernardino Tunnel (A13) provide 365-day all-weather highway transit beneath the high mountain passes.
What is the Tremola road on Passo del San Gottardo? ▼
The Tremola is the famous historic serpentine road paved with granite cobblestones climbing 300 vertical meters up the southern flank of Gotthard Pass from Airolo into Urseren.
Where can I check real-time TCS pass driving conditions for Ticino? ▼
LiveRoadStatus updates live road conditions directly from TCS (Touring Club Schweiz) at tcs.ch and alpenpaesse.ch.
