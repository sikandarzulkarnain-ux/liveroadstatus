Paso Visviri

Ruta CH-11 Extension • Visviri Border
• OPEN
Elevation: 4,175 m • Border: Chile – Bolivia

Northernmost international pass of Chile located near the tripartite border point of Chile, Peru, and Bolivia. Open daily for passenger traffic and local trade.

Paso Ancomarca

Unpaved Secondary Road • General Lagos
• CAUTION / SEASONAL
Elevation: 4,700 m • Border: Chile – Bolivia

Extremely remote high Andean crossing at 4,700 m altitude. Unpaved secondary gravel road; generally closed or restricted during winter snowpack and heavy summer rains.

Paso Colchane – Pisiga

Ruta CH-15 • Colchane to Oruro / Pisiga
• OPEN
Elevation: 3,715 m • Border: Chile – Bolivia

Major international commercial border pass connecting Iquique and Colchane with Pisiga and Oruro (Bolivia) across the high altiplano near Salar de Surire, Cariquima, and Volcán Isluga.

Invierno Altiplánico (Bolivian Winter) occurs between January and March, bringing heavy convective rain, thunderstorms, and high-altitude snowfall to the high Andes above 4,000 meters. Paso Chungará (4,520 m) may experience temporary closures or mandatory tire chain enforcement during these sudden storms.
