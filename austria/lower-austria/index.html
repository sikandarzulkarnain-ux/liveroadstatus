Frequently Asked Questions — Lower Austria Passes

Are mountain pass road conditions in Lower Austria updated live?
Yes. LiveRoadStatus updates road conditions, snow reports, and winter mandates for all 18 Lower Austria mountain passes every 5 minutes using official data streams from ÖAMTC and ASFINAG telemetry.
What winter equipment laws apply on Lower Austria passes?
Under Austrian law ('Situative Winterreifenpflicht' Nov 1 – Apr 15), winter tires with M+S marking are required whenever snow, slush, or ice is present. Steep passes such as Preiner Gscheid (1,070 m), Zellerrain (1,083 m), and Pielachtaler Gscheid (1,072 m) may mandate snow chains during active winter storms.
Are Semmering Pass, Wechsel Pass, and Rohrer Sattel open during winter?
Yes. Major state routes B17 (Semmering Pass), B54 (Wechsel Pass), and B21 (Rohrer Sattel & Kernhofer Gscheid) are prioritized for continuous snowplowing and anti-icing treatments, remaining open year-round except during severe emergency weather clearances.
