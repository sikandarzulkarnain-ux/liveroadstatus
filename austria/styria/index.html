Styria Mountain Passes: Frequently Asked Questions

Are Styria mountain pass driving conditions updated live? ▼
Yes. LiveRoadStatus updates road surface status, winter equipment laws, and avalanche closures for all 20 Styrian mountain passes every 5 minutes using official telemetry from ÖAMTC and ASFINAG.
When does Sölk Pass (Sölkpass) open and close for winter? ▼
Sölk Pass (1,788 m) on L701 across the Niedere Tauern is a high alpine pass subject to heavy winter snowpack and avalanche hazards. It typically closes in early November and reopens in mid-May following extensive snow-clearing operations.
What are the legal winter tire and snow chain mandates in Austria? ▼
In Austria, situational winter equipment is legally mandatory between November 1 and April 15 whenever roads are covered with snow, slush, or ice. Winter tires must have M+S or 3PMSF markings with a minimum tread depth of 4 mm for radials. Snow chains must be fitted on driven wheels when indicated by official road signs.
Which mountain passes connect Styria with neighboring Austrian states? ▼
Major inter-state passes include Pötschen Pass (993 m) and Hengst Pass (985 m) into Upper Austria; Semmering (984 m) and Wechsel (980 m) into Lower Austria; Weinebene (1,668 m), Packsattel (1,168 m), and Obdacher Sattel (1,374 m) into Carinthia; and Triebener Tauern (1,274 m) linking Upper Styria with Murtal.
What are the driving conditions on Triebener Tauern and Präbichl? ▼
Triebener Tauern (1,274 m) features steep 21% ramps and requires low gear engine braking and winter tires during snowstorms. Präbichl (1,226 m) is maintained open 365 days a year with active snowplow service serving the Eisenerz ski region.
