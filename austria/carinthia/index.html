Carinthia Mountain Passes: Frequently Asked Questions

Are Carinthia mountain pass driving conditions updated live? ▼
Yes. LiveRoadStatus updates road surface status, winter equipment laws, and border crossing alerts for all 20 Carinthian mountain passes every 5 minutes using official telemetry from ÖAMTC, ASFINAG, and DARS.
Which mountain passes in Carinthia connect into Slovenia and Italy? ▼
Carinthia features 6 international border passes: Wurzen Pass (1,073 m), Loibl Pass (1,368 m), Seebergsattel (1,218 m), and Paulitschsattel (1,338 m) cross into Slovenia; Nassfeld Pass (1,530 m) and Plöcken Pass (1,357 m) cross into Friuli, Italy.
What are the legal winter tire and snow chain mandates in Austria? ▼
In Austria, situational winter equipment is legally mandatory between November 1 and April 15 whenever roads are covered with snow, slush, or ice. Winter tires must have M+S or 3PMSF markings with a minimum tread depth of 4 mm for radials. Snow chains must be fitted on driven wheels when indicated by official road signs.
Is Nockalm Road (Schiestelscharte & Eisentalhöhe) open year-round? ▼
No. The Nockalm High Alpine Road (L68) across Schiestelscharte (2,022 m) and Eisentalhöhe (1,545 m) operates as a seasonal toll road through the Nockberge Biosphere Reserve, closing in early November and reopening after snow-clearing in early May.
What are the driving restrictions on Wurzen Pass and Turracher Höhe? ▼
Wurzen Pass features steep 18% inclines and bans vehicles towing heavy trailers. Turracher Höhe reaches 23% gradients on its northern approach; low gear engine braking and winter tires are essential during snowstorms.
