Montana Mountain Passes: Frequently Asked Questions

Direct verified answers for Montana pass road conditions, Glacier NP roads, and MDT status updates.

Going-to-the-Sun Road over Logan Pass (6,646 ft) in Glacier National Park is OPEN for the summer season (late June to mid-October). Closed in winter due to 80+ foot snowdrifts.

Beartooth Pass (10,947 ft) between Red Lodge, MT and Cooke City, MT is OPEN seasonally from Memorial Day weekend through mid-October. Sourced live from MDT.

Bozeman Pass (5,760 ft) and Homestake Pass (6,375 ft) on I-90 are primary Montana East-West freight corridors maintained 24/7 year-round by MDT.

Marias Pass (5,216 ft) along the southern boundary of Glacier NP and McDonald Pass (6,325 ft) near Helena are OPEN year-round with continuous MDT winter snowplowing.
