Alberta Mountain Passes: Frequently Asked Questions

What is the highest paved highway pass in Canada and when is Highwood Pass open? ▼
Highwood Pass on Highway 40 in Peter Lougheed Provincial Park (Kananaskis Country) is the highest paved highway pass in Canada at an elevation of 2,206 meters (7,238 feet). To protect wildlife migration corridors for bighorn sheep and grizzly bears, Highwood Pass is subject to an annual seasonal winter gate closure between December 1 and June 14.
What are winter tire requirements on Alberta highways and the Icefields Parkway? ▼
Winter tires (M+S or 3PMSF peak mountain snowflake symbol with at least 3.5 mm tread depth) or tire chains are legally mandatory on Highway 93 North (Icefields Parkway crossing Bow Pass and Sunwapta Pass) from November 1 to April 30. The Icefields Parkway has no cell phone service, no commercial snowplowing between 7 PM and 7 AM, and frequent avalanche control closures.
What permits are required for backcountry alpine pass treks in Banff & Jasper? ▼
Overnight wilderness trekking across high alpine passes such as Jonas Pass, Brazeau Pass, Boulder Pass (Skoki), Healy Pass, and Assiniboine Pass requires a Parks Canada Wilderness Pass and advance backcountry campsite reservations. Bear-resistant food canisters or hanging cables are mandatory.
Where can I check live Alberta 511 highway conditions? ▼
