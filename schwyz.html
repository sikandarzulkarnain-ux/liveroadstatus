Canton Schwyz Passes: Frequently Asked Questions

Is Ibergeregg Pass open year-round in Canton Schwyz? ▼
Yes. Ibergeregg Pass (1,406 m / 4,613 ft) connecting Oberiberg with Rickenbach / Schwyz is kept open 365 days a year by cantonal snowplows. However, during heavy winter snowstorms, 4WD or snow chains are required.
What are the road conditions on Sattelegg Pass? ▼
Sattelegg Pass (1,190 m) between Sihlsee (Willerzell) and Siebnen (Wägital) is an open year-round paved road. Black ice can occur during morning winter inversions.
When is Pragel Pass open for traffic? ▼
Pragel Pass (1,548 m) between Muotathal (Schwyz) and Netstal (Glarus) is closed during winter due to snow accumulation (typically November through May). In summer, motorized traffic is prohibited on Saturdays and Sundays on the Glarus side.
Is Sattel Pass open during heavy snowfall? ▼
Sattel Pass (932 m) along Hauptstrasse 8 is a major regional trunk road that receives continuous snowplow and de-icing service, keeping it reliably open year-round.

LiveRoadStatus automatically aggregates real-time road condition data, winter closure notices, and webcams from TCS (Touring Club Schweiz) at tcs.ch and Alpenpässe.ch.
