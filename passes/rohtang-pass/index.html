Current Mountain Pass Overview

High mountain pass connecting the Kullu Valley with the Lahaul and Spiti Valleys of Himachal Pradesh on the legendary Leh-Manali Highway.

Seasonal Schedule: Typically open May through November; closed in winter due to heavy snowpack.

Winter Equipment Requirements: 4WD/AWD or snow chains strictly enforced during snowfall; permit required from Manali administration.

Seasonal & Weather Notice: Road conditions change rapidly in high-altitude terrain. Traction tires or tire chains may be legally required during winter storm cycles.
