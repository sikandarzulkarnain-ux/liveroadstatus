Canton Graubünden / Grisons Passes: Frequently Asked Questions

Which Graubünden mountain passes are kept open year-round? ▼
Graubünden maintains 8 key mountain passes open 365 days a year with active snowplow operations: Julierpass (2,284 m), Berninapass (2,328 m), Malojapass (1,815 m), Ofenpass (2,149 m), Lukmanierpass (1,915 m), Lenzerheide (1,549 m), Wolfgangpass (1,631 m), and St. Luzisteig (713 m).
What is the highest paved road pass in Switzerland? ▼
Umbrail Pass (2,501 m / 8,205 ft) in Canton Graubünden near the Italian border is the highest paved road pass in Switzerland, connecting Val Müstair with Stelvio Pass.
