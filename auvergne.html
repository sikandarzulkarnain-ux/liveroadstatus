Frequently Asked Questions & Road Condition Advisories

Detailed answers for every mountain pass featured in the Auvergne-Rhône-Alpes pass map.

Status: Col de l'Iseran (2,770 m / 9,088 ft) on Route D902 is currently OPEN for summer and autumn transit between Val-d'Isère and Bonneval-sur-Arc. As the highest paved mountain pass in the Alps, it undergoes seasonal winter closure typically from early November through early June. Real-time updates are published by Savoie-Route and Info Route 73.

Status: Col de la Colombière (1,618 m / 5,308 ft) on Route D4 is OPEN today. Located in Haute-Savoie connecting Le Reposoir with Le Grand-Bornand, snow clearance teams maintain the pass during summer/fall. Winter closures run from mid-November to mid-May.

Status: Col des Aravis (1,487 m / 4,879 ft) on Route D909 is OPEN YEAR-ROUND. Linking La Clusaz in Haute-Savoie with Flumet in Savoie, continuous snowplow monitoring prevents seasonal shutdowns except during severe blizzard warnings.

Status: Col de la Forclaz (1,527 m / 5,010 ft) on Route D42 above Lake Annecy is OPEN TODAY. Kept open year-round with regular gritting during winter freeze.

Status: Col de Joux Plane (1,691 m / 5,548 ft) on Route D354 is OPEN TODAY. Connecting Morzine with Samoëns, this Tour de France mountain pass is subject to seasonal closure between late November and late April.

Status: Col de la Ramaz (1,619 m / 5,312 ft) on Route D308 is OPEN TODAY. Linking Mieussy with Sommand, winter conditions may trigger tunnel closures due to avalanche danger.

Status: Col de la Croix Fry (1,477 m / 4,846 ft) on Route D16 is OPEN YEAR-ROUND. Providing vital access between Manigod and La Clusaz ski fields, snowplows keep it cleared 365 days a year.

Status: Col de la Madeleine (1,993 m / 6,539 ft) on Route D213 is OPEN TODAY. Connecting La Léchère (Tarentaise) with La Chambre (Maurienne), it undergoes winter closure from November through May.

Status: Col de Voza (1,653 m / 5,423 ft) is RESTRICTED TO MOTOR VEHICLES. Located above Les Houches, it is open for hikers, mountain bikers, and users of the Tramway du Mont-Blanc.

Status: Col de la Seigne (2,516 m / 8,255 ft) is OPEN FOR TREKKING. As a key border pass on the Tour du Mont Blanc (TMB) connecting France and Italy, it is snowbound and impassable without mountaineering gear during winter and early spring.

Status: Col des Montets (1,461 m / 4,793 ft) on Route D1506 is OPEN YEAR-ROUND. Essential international trunk highway connecting Chamonix-Mont-Blanc with Vallorcine and Martigny (Switzerland). Snow canopy tunnels protect the road during winter.

Status: Col du Corbier (1,234 m / 4,049 ft) on Route D328 is OPEN YEAR-ROUND. Connecting Le Biot with Abondance valley in the Chablais region.

Status: Col de la Cou (1,116 m / 3,661 ft) on Route D12 is OPEN TODAY. Cleared regularly in winter connecting Faucigny with Samoëns.

Status: Col de la Givrine approaches (1,220 m / D1005 / Swiss Route 123) are OPEN YEAR-ROUND. Primary international border corridor connecting Saint-Cergue, La Cure, and Haute-Savoie.
