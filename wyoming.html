Frequently Asked Questions (Verified Pass Status)

Direct verified answers for Wyoming mountain pass road conditions and live open/closed status.

Beartooth Pass (elevation 10,947 ft / 3,345 m) on US-212 is OPEN for the summer season. Verified live via WYDOT 511. Road conditions are clear across the high alpine plateau. WYDOT closes the pass seasonally in winter from October to Memorial Day weekend.

Togwotee Pass (elevation 9,658 ft / 2,944 m) on US-26 / US-287 between Dubois and Jackson Hole is OPEN year-round. Sourced live from WYDOT 511. Pavement is clear across the Continental Divide with active winter snowplowing.

South Pass (elevation 7,550 ft / 2,300 m) on WY-28 is OPEN. Verified live via WYDOT 511. Highway 28 is clear; WYDOT level 1/2 chain laws operate during high wind and winter ground blizzards.

Sylvan Pass (elevation 8,524 ft / 2,600 m) on US-14/16/20 near Yellowstone East Entrance is OPEN. Sourced live from National Park Service and WYDOT 511. Open during the park summer season (May to November).

Teton Pass / Wind River Pass (elevation 8,431 ft / 2,570 m) on WY-22 connecting Jackson Hole, WY to Victor, ID is OPEN. Verified live via WYDOT 511. Steep 10% grades with active avalanche catchment barriers and winter mandatory traction laws.

Dunraven Pass (elevation 8,859 ft / 2,700 m) on Grand Loop Road in Yellowstone National Park is OPEN for the park summer season (May to October). Sourced live from NPS Yellowstone.
