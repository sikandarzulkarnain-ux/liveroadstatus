Mountain passes in India: common questions

Which is the highest motorable pass in India?
Chang La is the highest tracked pass in India, at about 5,360 m (17,585 ft).
When is the best time to cross high passes in India?
June to September is the primary window for most high Himalayan passes in Ladakh and Himachal Pradesh. Outside these months, snow and ice typically cause seasonal closures.
How often is pass status updated?
Statuses are refreshed typically every 5 to 60 minutes based on official Border Roads Organisation (BRO), police, and field transit reports.
Do I need a permit?
Yes, passes near international borders (such as Chang La, Khardung La, and Nathu La) require Inner Line Permits (ILP) or Protected Area Permits (PAP) for tourists.
