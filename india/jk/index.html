Frequently Asked Questions (J&K Pass Status)

Direct verified answers for Jammu & Kashmir mountain pass road conditions, live open/closed status, and BRO clearance updates.

Zoji La (elevation 11,575 ft / 3,528 m) on NH-1 connecting Kashmir Valley to Ladakh is OPEN. Border Roads Organisation (BRO Project Beacon & Vijayak) maintains priority snow clearing. Timed one-way convoy movements (Srinagar to Kargil / Kargil to Srinagar) are enforced daily by J&K Traffic Police.

Banihal Pass on the NH-44 Jammu-Srinagar National Highway is OPEN. Traffic bypasses the high-altitude pass summit via the 8.5 km Banihal Qazigund twin-tube tunnel and the classic Jawahar Tunnel.

Peer Ki Gali (elevation 11,450 ft / 3,490 m) on Mughal Road connecting Shopian to Bafliaz (Poonch/Rajouri) is OPEN during summer and autumn. Managed by J&K Mechanical Engineering & PWD with heavy winter snowplowing.

Razdan Pass (elevation 11,670 ft / 3,557 m) linking Bandipora to Gurez Valley is OPEN. Sourced live from BRO Project Beacon. High-power snow blowers clear Razdan Top during winter squalls.

Sinthan Pass (elevation 12,414 ft / 3,784 m) on NH-244 connecting Anantnag to Kishtwar is OPEN. Smooth paved double-lane highway maintained jointly by BRO and J&K PWD.
