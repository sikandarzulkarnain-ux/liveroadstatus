Frequently Asked Questions (Verified Pass Status)

Direct verified answers for Ladakh high mountain pass road conditions, live open/closed status, and BRO clearance updates.

Khardung La (elevation 17,582 ft / 5,359 m) connecting Leh to Nubra Valley is OPEN. Verified live via Border Roads Organisation (BRO Project Himank). Pavement is cleared daily by heavy snow blowers. During winter snow squalls, snow chains or 4WD vehicles are enforced at South Pullu checkpoint.

Chang La (elevation 17,590 ft / 5,360 m) connecting Leh to Pangong Tso is OPEN. Sourced live from BRO Himank. The steep asphalt switchbacks are plowed year-round. Drivers should watch for early morning black ice near the summit prayer flag stupa.

Tanglang La (elevation 17,480 ft / 5,328 m) on the Leh-Manali Highway (NH-3) is OPEN. Smooth paved 2-lane highway across the Zanskar Range crest, maintained with priority winter snowplowing by BRO.

Lachulung La (16,616 ft) and Nakee La (15,547 ft) on NH-3 between Pang and Sarchu are OPEN. The double-lane highway over Gata Loops is clear for commercial trucks and passenger SUVs.

Morey Plains (elevation 15,400 ft / 4,690 m) on NH-3 is OPEN. The 40 km long smooth high-desert plateau highway allows standard cruising speed, but drivers should prepare for strong crosswinds and sand squalls.

Fotu La (13,478 ft) and Namika La (12,139 ft) on the Srinagar-Leh Highway (NH-1) are OPEN year-round. Sourced live from BRO Project Vijayak. They represent the smoothest paved passes in Ladakh with minimal winter downtime.

Pensi La (elevation 14,436 ft / 4,400 m) on the Kargil-Padum road (NH-301) in Zanskar is OPEN for summer/autumn travel. BRO Vijayak clears the pass from May to November; winter closures occur from December through April due to heavy snow near Drang-Drung Glacier.

Marsimik La (elevation 18,314 ft / 5,582 m) is RESTRICTED. Travel requires an Inner Line Permit (ILP) and Indian Army border clearance. High-clearance 4WD vehicles are mandatory for the unpaved steep gravel trail.

Umling La (elevation 19,024 ft / 5,798 m) on the Chisumle-Demchok road is OPEN. As the world's highest paved motorable pass (Guinness World Record), oxygen levels are less than 50% of sea level. Drivers must acclimatize in Leh for at least 48 hours and carry supplementary oxygen canisters.

Shingo La (Shinkula) (elevation 16,580 ft / 5,054 m) connecting Zanskar to Lahaul is OPEN for summer travel. BRO Project Yojak maintains snow clearance; construction of the 4.1 km Shingo La tunnel is active to provide all-weather connectivity.

Kongka Pass (Kongka La) (elevation 16,965 ft / 5,171 m) in Chang Chenmo Valley is RESTRICTED. Located directly on the Line of Actual Control (LAC) with Aksai Chin; restricted to Indian Armed Forces with no civilian tourist access.
